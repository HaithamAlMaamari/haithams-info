01Automation system

Personal project / active

A daily news briefing that checks itself before it arrives.

A cron-scheduled pipeline gathers news from multiple sources, validates what deserves attention, and turns the final selection into a concise daily briefing.

Phase 01

Gather

Sweeps Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 sources to build a candidate list for the daily edition.

Five mandatory checks

V1 - V5
  1. Source verificationOpens each URL, checks the date, and reads the article content.
  2. Cross-referenceTop-priority stories need independent confirmation or are qualified or removed.
  3. Historical deduplicationChecks the previous three issues before repeating a story.
  4. Internal consistencyRechecks summaries, URLs, issue duplicates, and the 12-story limit.
  5. Editorial noteRecords what passed, what was dropped, and why.

DeepSeek analyses coverage, validates the daily selection, and prepares the final delivery message for the chosen channel.
